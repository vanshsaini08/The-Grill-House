About The Grill House

Home of the Flame-Grilled Perfection — Since 1954

Our Story

Flame-Grilled
Perfection

Every great story starts with fire. Since 1954, The Grill House has been committed to flame-grilling every single burger patty over an open flame. It's not just a process — it's a promise that gives our burgers that smoky, authentic flavour you won't find anywhere else.

In India, we opened our first restaurant in 2014 and have been serving millions of happy customers with our signature taste, bold flavours, and unbeatable value.

Quality First

Fresh Ingredients,
Always

At The Grill House, we believe that great taste starts with great ingredients. Our buns are freshly baked, our lettuce is crisp, tomatoes are vine-ripened, and our onions are hand-cut. We use 100% real dairy paneer and cheese — because you deserve nothing less.

Every ingredient is sourced with care and undergoes rigorous quality checks before it reaches your plate. That's the Grill House promise.
